# Core Banking Glossary

| **Term** | **Definition** |
|---|---|
| **Aggregate** | A consistency boundary with one aggregate root controlling changes to its internal entities. |
| **Applicant** | A person or organization being evaluated during onboarding before becoming an active customer. |
| **Authoritative record** | The capability and data set permitted to change a specific business fact. |
| **Authorization hold** | Temporary reservation created during authorization that may later clear, expire, reverse, or be released. |
| **Available balance** | Amount currently available after holds, restrictions, limits, and applicable settlement rules. |
| **Business date** | The institution's operational date, which may differ from the wall-clock date. |
| **Business event** | An immutable statement that a meaningful, committed domain change occurred. |
| **Card instrument** | Physical, virtual, tokenized, debit, prepaid, charge, or credit card access instrument linked to an account or credit arrangement. |
| **Clearing** | Exchange of payment or card transaction details through a scheme, processor, gateway, or clearing provider before final settlement. |
| **Command** | A request to perform a state-changing business operation. |
| **Contract** | A customer-specific financial agreement created from product terms and subsequent approved changes. |
| **Days Past Due (DPD)** | Number of days an unpaid obligation has remained overdue under the applicable policy. |
| **Due diligence** | Evidence collection, verification, screening, and decisioning required before or during a customer relationship. |
| **Effective dating** | Modeling when a version, rate, rule, or assignment becomes and ceases to be applicable. |
| **Exposure** | Amount currently drawn, reserved, committed, pending, or otherwise at risk across one or more customers, groups, accounts, cards, or facilities. |
| **Finality** | Point at which a payment or settlement outcome is no longer expected to be reversed under the applicable rules, except through a new correction. |
| **General Ledger** | Institution-wide books of account containing posted journal lines and balances. |
| **Idempotency** | Property that repeated processing of the same request does not duplicate its business effect. |
| **Journal entry** | Balanced set of debit and credit lines representing an accounting consequence. |
| **KYC / KYB** | Know Your Customer or Know Your Business processes used to identify, verify, screen, and risk-assess customers or organizations. |
| **Ledger balance** | Posted accounting or subledger amount before availability restrictions or pending items. |
| **Limit** | Approved constraint on utilization, availability, exposure, authorization, overdraft, facility usage, or transaction activity. |
| **Onboarding case** | Controlled lifecycle record for collecting evidence, performing checks, resolving exceptions, and deciding whether to create a customer. |
| **Payment orchestration** | Selection and control of validation, routing, provider handoff, scheme exchange, exception handling, and settlement tracking for a payment. |
| **Payment repair** | Controlled correction of operational payment details before or during scheme processing without obscuring the original instruction. |
| **Product** | Reusable template of commercial, policy, accounting, and operational terms. |
| **Product simulation** | Evidence-producing test of product behavior before activation, migration, or mass change. |
| **Projection or read model** | Derived data optimized for queries and reporting, not authoritative state changes. |
| **Recall** | Request to cancel or retrieve a payment after submission according to the applicable scheme or provider rules. |
| **Reversal** | Explicit compensating operation that preserves the original record and negates its effect. |
| **Scheme exchange** | Outbound or inbound message interaction with a payment scheme, clearing network, processor, or gateway. |
| **Screening referral** | Request and response context for sanctions, PEP, adverse-media, fraud, AML, or other financial-crime checks. |
| **Settlement** | Completion evidence that value movement has been finalized between internal accounts, providers, schemes, banks, or settlement accounts. |
| **Subledger** | Detailed operational ledger for a product domain, such as loan or savings transactions. |
| **Value date** | Date from which a financial transaction affects interest or economic value. |
| **Workflow instance** | A running, version-bound execution of a business process definition. |
| **Write-off** | Recognition that an exposure is considered a loss for accounting or operational purposes; it does not necessarily extinguish legal recovery rights. |
